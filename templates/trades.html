Completed trades

Each trade is graded twice. At the time: value is what the market says you gained or gave up, and fit is how much of it reached your starting lineup. Afterwards: the season is replayed with the trade undone, keeping every lineup decision you actually made and refilling only the slots the trade vacated — so the swing is in points that really would have been on your board, and a won or lost game can honestly be recomputed.

One known bias: the replay keeps every waiver pickup you really made, including ones you made to cover the hole a trade created — so it hands your traded-away player back and keeps his replacement, when you only ever had one of the two. That makes the pre-trade roster look slightly too strong, which means every trade here reads a little less valuable than it actually was.
