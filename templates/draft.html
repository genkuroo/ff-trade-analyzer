Every pick, graded against the market as it stands today. The number in the corner of each card is pick minus ADP — negative (red) means the team reached for him ahead of where the market drafts him; positive (green) means he lasted and they got him later than expected. Hover a card for the exact numbers.

This is the instant read, the same idea as the trade machine's instant grade — it says nothing about who actually ended up with the better players, because that needs a season of real scoring. A retrospective pass — who got the best players out of their draft — arrives once games have been played; for now this is recorded as the pre-season baseline to compare against later.
