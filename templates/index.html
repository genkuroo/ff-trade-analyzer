Ranked by how they have actually played

A fantasy record is part team, part draw. All-play asks a different question: each week, how many of the other nine teams would you have beaten? Over a season that deletes the schedule, because everyone faces the same opponents — all of them, every week. The gap between the wins a team has and the wins their scoring earned is luck, in the literal sense of results that had nothing to do with how they played.
